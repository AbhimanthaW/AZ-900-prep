**traditional corporate datacenter**
- Company
	- maintaining the physical space, ensuring security, and maintaining or replacing the servers if anything happens
- IT department
	- maintaining all the infrastructure and software needed to keep the datacenter up and running, keeping all systems patched and on the correct version
