
### 1. Reservations
- Best for **stable, predictable workloads**
- Commit to specific resource capacity
- **1- or 3-year** term
- Discounted pricing for matching usage

### 2. Azure Savings Plan for Compute
- Best for **steady but flexible compute usage**
- Commit to an **hourly spend amount**
- **1- or 3-year** term
- Applies savings across eligible compute services
    

### 3. Spot Pricing
- Uses **unused Azure capacity**
- Significantly reduced pricing
- Azure can reclaim capacity → workloads may be interrupted
- Best for **fault-tolerant / interruptible workloads**

### Quick Decision

|Workload|Best option|
|---|---|
|Predictable, long-running, stable resources|**Reservations**|
|Steady usage with compute flexibility|**Savings Plan**|
|Interruptible / fault-tolerant, lowest cost priority|**Spot**|

- **Review regularly:** Change pricing model as workload requirements and usage patterns change
- Stable workloads → consider commitment pricing
- Business-critical workloads → avoid interruptible Spot capacity
