**Core Concept**
- **External Identity:** Any user, device, or service existing outside your primary tenant (partners, suppliers, consumers).
- **Identity vs. Authorization:** External identity providers handle authentication (Bring Your Own Identity); Microsoft Entra ID manages authorization to your applications.

**External ID Capabilities**

|**Capability**|**Target Audience**|**Directory Presence**|**Best Used For**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**B2B Collaboration**|External partners, vendors, contractors|Represented in tenant (usually as **guest users**)|Sharing access to internal/SaaS apps, files, and resources via guest invitations.|
|**B2B Direct Connect**|External Microsoft Entra tenants|**Not** represented in local directory|Seamless cross-tenant collaboration (e.g., Microsoft Teams shared channels).|
|**External ID for Customers** _(formerly Azure AD B2C)_|Consumers & business customers|Managed in dedicated external tenant|Customer Identity & Access Management (CIAM) for custom or modern SaaS apps.|

**Guest Access Lifecycle Management**

- **Access Request & Onboarding:** Managed via admin invites, self-service registration flows, or Entitlement Management workflows.
    
- **Access Reviews:** Periodic recertification by external users or decision-makers to confirm ongoing need.
    
- **Deprovisioning:** Automatic or manual removal of access for guests who fail recertification or no longer require access.
