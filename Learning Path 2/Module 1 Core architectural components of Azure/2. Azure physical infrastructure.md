Datacenters - servers arranged in racks
-  dedicated power, cooling, and networking infrastructure

datacenters are grouped into Azure Regions and Azure Availability Zones
- resiliency and reliability

**Global Infrastructure**

|   Geography   | Region  | Availability Zone | Datacenter |
| :-----------: | :-----: | :---------------: | :--------: |
| United States | East US |    3+ / region    |   server   |

geography - data residency and compliance boundary
region - datacenters networked with low latency
availability zones - connected through high-speed, private fiber-optic networks

**Azure Services that support Availability Zones**
1. Zonal Services - pin resource to specific zone
2. Zone-redundant service - platform replicates automatically across zones
	- zone-redundant storage, SQL Database
3. Non-regional services - always available from Azure geographies

**Region Pairs**
regions are paired with another region within the same geography at least 300 miles away
- West US x East US / Southeast Asia x East Asia
- reliable services and data redundancy

exceptions:
- no regional pairs: Italy North, Poland Central, and Israel Central
- pair outside geography: Brazil South x South Central US

**Sovereign Regions**
isolated from the main instance of Azure

1. US DoD Central, US Gov Virginia, US Gov Arizona etc
	- U.S. government agencies and partners

2. China East, China North
	* Microsoft and 21Vianet partneship
